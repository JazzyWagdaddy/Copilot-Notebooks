# Northwind Trail Visitor Feedback — Pilot Discovery

## Research summary

The product team reviewed feedback from 18 recent visitors and five customer-support conversations. The feedback was collected to identify needs for a possible mobile route-planning pilot. The sample is directional and should not be treated as representative of all visitors.

## Recurring needs

### Offline access

Eleven visitors said they had weak or inconsistent mobile service during at least part of their trip. Several wanted to download route details before leaving the visitor center.

### Clear difficulty guidance

Nine visitors said route descriptions did not give enough detail about elevation, surface conditions, or suitability for children. Two visitors changed routes after arriving because the selected route was more difficult than expected.

### Current conditions

Seven visitors wanted more confidence that closure, maintenance, and weather-related guidance was current. Customer-support conversations also included questions about how frequently trail information is updated.

## Positive feedback

Visitors responded positively to the idea of:

- A short route comparison
- A downloadable checklist
- Safety reminders shown before starting a route

## Concerns

- Four visitors worried that a mobile experience could encourage people to look at a screen while walking.
- Three visitors asked whether the experience would collect precise location data.
- Two visitors said they would prefer a printable option.

## Research gaps

The discovery work did not establish:

- An approved product budget
- A preferred launch date
- The three routes that should be included
- Accessibility requirements
- Whether users expect turn-by-turn navigation
