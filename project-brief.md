# Northwind Trail Mobile Route Pilot — Project Brief

## Purpose

Northwind Trail wants to test whether a mobile route-planning experience can help visitors prepare for and complete self-guided trail activities with greater confidence.

## Target users

The initial pilot is intended for:

- First-time visitors to Northwind Trail
- Families planning half-day activities
- Visitors who expect inconsistent mobile connectivity

## Proposed pilot scope

The pilot will focus on three capabilities:

1. Pre-trip route selection
2. Downloadable route details for offline access
3. Safety reminders and trail-condition guidance

Social sharing, reservations, payments, and live location tracking are outside the initial pilot scope.

## Proposed timing

The team proposes a six-week pilot beginning after the final content and safety review. The brief does not provide an approved calendar start date.

## Proposed success measures

- At least 60% of enrolled pilot users download a route before arrival.
- At least 70% of surveyed users report that route information was easy to understand.
- Fewer than 10% of surveyed users report being unable to access essential route details during their visit.

## Known dependencies

- Trail Operations must approve safety language.
- The Content team must validate route descriptions.
- The pilot team needs a decision on how frequently trail-condition guidance will be refreshed.

## Open decisions

- Which three routes should be included?
- Who owns trail-condition updates during the pilot?
- What is the approved pilot start date?
- What budget is available for user research and support?
